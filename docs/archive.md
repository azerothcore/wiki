# Archive

This is the list of wiki pages that are no longer maintained.

Pages end up here when they are outdated or no longer apply to AzerothCore, but still hold enough historical or reference value that deleting them would be a loss. They are not linked from anywhere else in the wiki and every one of them shows an archive notice at the top, so nobody follows them by accident.

Archived pages keep the same url they had before they were archived, so old links from outside the wiki still work.

See [wiki standards](wiki-standards#archived-pages) for how to archive a page.
